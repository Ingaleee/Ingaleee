## About me

I'm a **Senior C#/.NET backend engineer with 4+ years of production experience** across industrial platforms, cybersecurity and fintech. I build systems that remain reliable under load, partial failures and changing requirements.

My focus is **performance, reliability, clean architecture and measurable impact**: distributed systems, event-driven processing, payments, observability and production support. I take ownership from architecture and implementation through deployment, monitoring and incident resolution.

**Experience:** Wilo SE · Sber Cybersecurity · EGAR International · Geropharm
